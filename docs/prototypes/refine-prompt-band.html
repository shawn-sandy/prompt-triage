What to validate

  2. After Refine, is the rewrite in the box and the issue list easy to find under Show issues?
  3. Does Restore original put back exactly what you typed?
  4. On the first prompt, choose Refine prompt in the model dialog under both message settings. Is the one-line notice clearer than the red warning?
